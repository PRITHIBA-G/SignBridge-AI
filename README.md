# SignBridge AI – Breaking Communication Barriers

SignBridge AI is an AI-powered, inclusive communication platform designed to bridge the communication gap between people who use sign language and people who do not understand it.

The platform supports two-way communication by converting recognized hand gestures into text and providing speech-to-text and text-to-speech capabilities. It integrates Gnani AI APIs to support voice-based interactions and multilingual communication.

### Key Features
- **Sign Language Recognition:** Recognizes predefined hand gestures through a webcam and converts them into text.
- **Speech-to-Text:** Converts spoken communication into written text.
- **Text-to-Speech:** Converts typed text into speech.
- **Multilingual Support:** Supports communication in English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, and Malayalam.
- **Real-Time Interaction:** Provides webcam-based gesture recognition and voice interaction.
